Developed a Tableau dashboard to analyze Adidas retail sales and present key business insights in a clear, accessible format.
The project focused on organizing sales data, analyzing performance across products and geographic locations, and tracking sales trends over different time periods.
Key work:
- Prepared and cleaned sales data for analysis.
- Analyzed sales performance by product, region, and salesperson.
- Created KPI cards and interactive visualizations to summarize key metrics.
- Explored sales trends across dates and time periods.
- Designed a user-friendly dashboard to support data exploration and reporting.
Tools: Tabluea.
The final dashboard helps users explore sales performance and understand patterns through interactive visuals and filters.
